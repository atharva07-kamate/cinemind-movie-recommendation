REPRODUCIBLE METHOD

How to read these numbers

01Hold out ratings

Random 80/20 split (fixed seed {{ result.seed }}): {{ '{:,}'.format(result.train_ratings) }} training and {{ '{:,}'.format(result.test_ratings) }} test ratings.

02Find relevant items

Test-set ratings of {{ result.relevance_threshold }} stars or more count as relevant. {{ result.users_evaluated }} users with held-out relevant items are evaluated.

03Rank & compare

User-based cosine collaborative filtering ranks unseen training movies. Precision and recall are macro-averaged over evaluated users.

These are offline estimates on MovieLens latest-small; a random split can include interactions from the same period in both sets and does not guarantee real-world performance.
