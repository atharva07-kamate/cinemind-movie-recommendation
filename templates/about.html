MODEL NOTES

Why these techniques?

Content-based

TF-IDF encodes genres and cosine similarity compares their direction. The result finds titles with similar genre composition.

Collaborative

Users with similar rating vectors vote on unseen movies. It can surface preferences beyond literal genre matches.

Popularity prior

Bayesian shrinkage blends a movie's average with the global mean, reducing the influence of sparse rating counts.
