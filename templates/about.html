WHY THESE METHODS?

Signals behind the ranking

Content-based

TF-IDF encodes genre features. Cosine similarity compares feature-vector direction to find titles with similar genre composition.

Collaborative

Cosine similarity compares user rating patterns. Ratings from similar profiles contribute to unseen-title scores.

Popularity prior

Bayesian shrinkage blends a movie mean with the dataset mean to lower the influence of sparse ratings.

The dataset records ratings. CineMind describes these as rating history and does not claim that a rating proves a movie was watched.
